# CtaCard

The full-width call-to-action block at the bottom of marketing pages.

**Consumer provides:** `title`, optional `eyebrow`, body `children`, `actions` (usually one primary and one outline Button).

1px `accent` border, `radius-cta` (12px), `cta-card-padding` (40px), a faint gold wash from left (9.5% to 3%). Use once per page.

Source: DESIGN.md `card-cta`.
