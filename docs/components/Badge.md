# Badge

Small pill label for "Most Popular" on fleet cards and similar flags.

**Consumer provides:** `children` (one or two words), `variant` (`solid` default, or `soft`).

`solid`: `accent` fill, `cod-900` text, `radius-full`, 12px/600. `soft`: `accent-fill` with `accent-text`. For trip states in the driver app use StatusPill, not Badge.

Source: DESIGN.md `badge-pill`.
