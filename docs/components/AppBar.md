# AppBar

Driver-app top bar, 56px (`app-bar-h`), sticky.

**Consumer provides:** `title`, `back` + `onBack` on detail screens, or `brand` on the home screen, and an optional `right` slot (avatar, menu).

`bg-app` with a bottom hairline. The brand lockup is text: "PAL·Driver" with a gold middle dot. Content is capped at `container-app` (520px).

Source: driver `app-bar.tsx`.
