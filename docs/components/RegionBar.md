# RegionBar

Country filter with live clocks, as in the WeWeb top bar.

**Products:** Back office.

**Consumer provides:** `v-model` (country id, 0 for all countries).

Local time for Singapore and Thailand in Geist Mono with the city above it, then the service country as a MultiSelect in single mode (170px, no search row): All countries or one country, with a check on the chosen one. The clocks refresh every 30 seconds. Used on Operations, Drivers, Vehicles and Settings.

Source: `PAL Backend/web/src/components/list/RegionBar.vue`.
