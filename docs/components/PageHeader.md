# PageHeader

Title row at the top of a list page, with a one-line summary and the page actions.

**Products:** Back office.

**Consumer provides:** `title`, optional `subtitle` (such as "124 bookings in view"), actions in the default slot.

24px 700 title in `text-primary`, 14px `text-muted` subtitle, actions aligned to the right and wrapping under the title on narrow screens. At most one gold button among the actions. Operations puts its city clocks and country select here too. shadcn has no page header; this one follows its type scale.

Source: `PAL Backend/web/src/components/list/PageHeader.vue`.
